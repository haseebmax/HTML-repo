order to bake a cake

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Grease and flour a cake pan.
  3. In a mixing bowl, combine the dry ingredients (flour, sugar, baking powder, salt).
  4. In another bowl, mix the wet ingredients (eggs, milk, vanilla extract, melted butter).
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until smooth.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan.
  7. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  8. Allow the cake to cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
